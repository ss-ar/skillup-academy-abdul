About SkillUp Academy

Discover our story, mission, and learning culture

Who We Are

SkillUp Academy is an innovative practical training hub located in Kampala, Uganda. Founded with the vision of making quality ICT education accessible to everyone, we train students using 100% practical curriculum models.

Whether you are a complete beginner seeking computer literacy or a professional adding web development and networking to your resume, we guide you step-by-step.

Our Mission

To equip youth and career seekers with practical, job-ready digital skills through accessible, affordable, and intensive hands-on instruction.

Our Vision

To become the leading community digital skills ecosystem in East Africa, transforming passionate learners into skilled tech leaders.

Why Learn With Us?

100% Practical Training

No boring theory. Every class takes place inside a fully equipped computer lab.

Flexible Schedules

Choose morning, afternoon, or Saturday weekend classes that fit your daily schedule.

Practical Certificate

Earn a verified certificate of completion after presenting your final practical project.
